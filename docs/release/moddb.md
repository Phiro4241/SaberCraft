## Beschreibung (BBCode/HTML-Editor von ModDB, als Text einfügen)

Derselbe Inhalt wie das README und die Modrinth-Beschreibung, nur als schlichter Text.

```
SaberCraft brings the lightsaber to Minecraft, and builds a galaxy around it. Ignite a blade that hums, glows and clashes like in the films, fight with fifteen styles and the Force, call your droids, climb into an X-Wing or walk the corridors of a Star Destroyer. And when you are ready, slip aboard the Tenebris in "Nightshade", a six-mission stealth story that ends with a lightsaber duel.

SaberCraft is an unofficial, free fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Lucasfilm, Disney, Mojang or Microsoft. It contains no film music, film sounds or film artwork: every model, texture, sound and piece of music was made for this mod.

LIGHTSABERS AND COMBAT

- Turn any sword you choose into a lightsaber in the SaberCraft menu (key I). It ignites, hums as you move, leaves a trail, throws sparks where blades meet and turns back into exactly the sword it was.
- 14 hilts and 6 blade colors; each color's core, glow, length and flicker can be tuned.
- 15 fighting styles, guard, parry, dodges, a spin attack, and blaster bolts turned back with the blade.
- The Force: push, grip, pull and jump. Works with Iris shader packs.

THE STORY: CHAPTER 1, "NIGHTSHADE"

For three weeks Kestrel, an agent hidden aboard the Star Destroyer Tenebris, sent data to the Rebellion. Then one last message: "Nightshade is not a ship. It is a hunt." You are smuggled aboard to find her, and to learn what Nightshade is.

- Six missions, from a briefing on the Millennium Falcon to an escape from a ship that is tearing itself apart.
- Stay in the dark: the crew notices you in stages, troopers chase you, call for help and search where they last saw you. Crawl through vents, take down an unaware guard from behind, let your droid helpers make a noise or open a lock, and walk among the troops in stormtrooper armour.
- The characters speak to you by your own Minecraft name, and a choice you make changes what comes later.
- The finale: troopers who open fire the moment they see you, then a duel against the Shadowblade and her spinning ring saber.
- Checkpoints, three difficulties (Easy, Normal, Hard), skippable cutscenes, its own music for every situation, English and German.

STARSHIPS

- X-Wing, TIE Fighter and TIE Interceptor to fly, with lasers, a flight HUD and a chase camera.
- Millennium Falcon and Star Destroyer to fly and to walk through: cockpit, lounge, gun turrets, a bridge, a hangar, a reactor and a crew at work.
- Hull and flight energy; crashes break no blocks.

DROIDS, BLASTERS AND MORE

- 8 droid companions (R2-D2, C-3PO, BB-8, Chopper, a mouse droid, a probe droid, a B1 battle droid, K-2SO), each with moving parts, a wordless voice and an ability.
- 9 blasters that turn a bow into a blaster and fire plain arrows as ammunition.
- 11 film characters and 5 neutral outfits with original pixel skins, plus your own profiles.
- A space sky at night and 6 planet skies (Tatooine, Coruscant, Endor, Hoth, Mustafar, Naboo); the terrain stays yours.
- 30 original pieces of music that follow what happens, always crossfaded. A separate music mode plays music files you add yourself.
- Voluntary lightsaber duels between players and 11 advancements.

REQUIREMENTS

- Minecraft Java Edition 26.2 (other versions are not supported)
- Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer
- Fabric API for 26.2 (built and tested with 0.161.0+26.2)

INSTALLATION

1. Install Fabric Loader for Minecraft 26.2 with the Fabric installer (fabricmc.net).
2. Put Fabric API for 26.2 and sabercraft-0.1.0.jar into the "mods" folder of your Minecraft installation.
3. Start the Fabric profile. Take a sword, press I and click "Make lightsaber". The story is the first tab of the same menu.

On a server, install SaberCraft and Fabric API on the server and on every client. Each player plays their own copy of the story.

GOOD TO KNOW

- Only Minecraft 26.2 with Fabric; no Forge, NeoForge or Quilt.
- Chapter 1 of the story is complete; there are no further chapters yet.
- Starships, droids and blasters are the newest parts and have not been balanced with many players yet.
- A server full of real players at once has not been tried yet.

HOW IT WAS MADE

SaberCraft was developed with extensive help from an AI coding assistant, directed, reviewed and play-tested by the author. All models, textures, sounds and music are produced by the project's own scripts; no images, sounds or music come from AI image or music generators or from the films.

LINKS

Source code, all controls, commands and settings, bug reports: https://github.com/Phiro4241/SaberCraft
Support the project (voluntary, unlocks nothing): https://patreon.com/Phiro4241?utm_medium=unknown&utm_source=join_link&utm_campaign=creatorshare_creator&utm_content=copyLink

License: MIT (source code and the mod's own assets). Star Wars and all related names are trademarks of their owners; the license grants no rights to them.
```
