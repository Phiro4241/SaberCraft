## Beschreibung (BBCode/HTML-Editor von ModDB, als Text einfügen)

```
SaberCraft turns Minecraft's swords into film-style lightsabers and builds a whole galaxy around them: fighting styles and Force abilities, blasters, droid companions, starships you can fly and walk through, planet skies, original music and a story campaign.

SaberCraft is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Lucasfilm, Disney, Mojang or Microsoft. It contains no film music, no film sounds and no film artwork: every model, texture, sound and piece of music was made for this project.

STORY MODE: CHAPTER 1, "NIGHTSHADE"

For three weeks an agent hidden aboard the Star Destroyer Tenebris sent data to the Rebellion. Then one last message: "Nightshade is not a ship. It is a hunt." You are smuggled aboard to find her and to learn what Nightshade is.

- Six missions: a briefing aboard the Millennium Falcon, sneaking aboard, a disguise, the reactor, a discovery, and a finale with an escape through a failing ship and a lightsaber duel.
- Stealth: darkness, cover, sneaking, crawl ways and silent takedowns. Troopers notice you in stages, chase you, call for help and search where they last saw you.
- A stormtrooper disguise, dialogs with choices, two droid helpers and short cutscenes you can skip.
- The finale: troopers who fire as soon as they see you, then a duel in phases against the Shadowblade, a fighter with a spinning ring saber.
- The characters speak to you by your own Minecraft name.
- Checkpoints, three difficulties (Easy, Normal, Hard), music for every situation, in German and English.

FEATURES

- Lightsabers: ignition and retraction, glow, hum and swing sounds. 14 hilts, 6 blade colors, 15 fighting styles, guard, parry, dodges, a spin attack and four Force abilities (push, grip, pull, jump). Only the swords you choose become sabers. Works with Iris shader packs.
- Characters: 11 film characters and 5 neutral outfits with original pixel skins, plus your own profiles.
- Blasters (experimental): 9 blasters that use plain arrows as ammunition, with heat and recoil.
- Droids (experimental): 8 droid companions with moving parts, wordless synthesized voices and one ability each.
- Starships (experimental): a flyable X-Wing, TIE Fighter and TIE Interceptor; a Millennium Falcon and a Star Destroyer you can fly and walk through.
- Skies: a space sky and 6 planet skies (sky and sound only, the terrain stays as it is).
- Music: 30 original pieces that follow what happens, with crossfades. A "Star Wars" mode plays only music files you add yourself.
- Voluntary duels between players (best of three) and 11 advancements.

REQUIREMENTS

- Minecraft Java Edition 26.2 (other versions are not supported)
- Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer
- Fabric API (required)

INSTALLATION

1. Install Fabric Loader for Minecraft 26.2 with the Fabric installer (fabricmc.net).
2. Put Fabric API for 26.2 and sabercraft-0.1.0.jar into the "mods" folder of your Minecraft installation.
3. Start the Fabric profile. Take a sword, press I and click "Make lightsaber". The story is in the same menu.

On a server, install SaberCraft and Fabric API on the server and on every client. Each player plays their own copy of the story.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS

- Only Minecraft 26.2 with Fabric. No Forge, NeoForge or Quilt.
- Only Chapter 1 of the story exists.
- Blasters, droids and starships are experimental: little played by hand and not balanced with real players.
- A server with several real players at once has not been tested yet.

HOW IT WAS MADE

SaberCraft was developed with extensive help from an AI coding assistant, directed, reviewed and play-tested by the author. All models, textures, sounds and music are generated procedurally by the project's own scripts; no images, sounds or music come from AI generators or from the films.

LINKS

Source code, documentation and bug reports: https://github.com/Phiro4241/SaberCraft
Support the project (voluntary, unlocks nothing): PATREON_URL_EINTRAGEN

License: MIT (source code and the mod's own assets). Star Wars and all related names are trademarks of their owners; the license grants no rights to them.
```

**Titel:** `SaberCraft 0.1.0: lightsabers and a stealth story for Minecraft 26.2`

**Zusammenfassung:** `The first public version of SaberCraft is here: film-style lightsabers and Chapter 1 of a six-mission story aboard a Star Destroyer, for Minecraft Java 26.2 with Fabric.`

**Text:**

```
SaberCraft 0.1.0 is the first public version of my Fabric mod for Minecraft Java 26.2.

Any sword you choose can become a lightsaber that ignites, glows, hums and retracts, with 14 hilts, 6 blade colors, 15 fighting styles and four Force abilities. Around the sabers there are blasters that fire arrows, droid companions, starships, planet skies and 30 original pieces of music.

The heart of this version is the story mode: Chapter 1, "Nightshade". You are smuggled aboard the Star Destroyer Tenebris to find a missing agent. Six missions of stealth, disguise, dialogs and droid help lead to the reactor, a discovery in a hidden lab and a duel against the Shadowblade. The characters speak to you by your own Minecraft name, and you can choose between three difficulties.

SaberCraft is an unofficial fan project with its own models, sounds and music. It needs Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer and Fabric API.

Download, source code and bug reports: https://github.com/Phiro4241/SaberCraft
Feedback is very welcome. Have fun aboard the Tenebris!
```
