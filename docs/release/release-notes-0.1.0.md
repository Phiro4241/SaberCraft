# SaberCraft 0.1.0

The first public version of SaberCraft for **Minecraft Java 26.2** with Fabric: film-style lightsabers, a galaxy around them, and the complete first chapter of a story campaign.

SaberCraft is an unofficial, free fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Lucasfilm, Disney, Mojang or Microsoft. It contains no film music, film sounds or film artwork.

## Requirements

- Minecraft Java Edition **26.2**
- **Fabric Loader** 0.19.5 or newer
- **Fabric API** for 26.2 (built and tested with 0.161.0+26.2)

In single player the mod is all you need. On a server, install SaberCraft and Fabric API on the server and on every client.

## What is in it

**The story: Chapter 1, "Nightshade"**

- Six missions aboard the Star Destroyer *Tenebris*, from the briefing on the Millennium Falcon to an escape and a lightsaber duel against the *Shadowblade*.
- Stealth in stages, darkness and cover, crawl ways, silent takedowns, a stormtrooper disguise, dialogs with a choice that matters, and two droid helpers.
- The characters speak to you by your own Minecraft name.
- Checkpoints, three difficulties, skippable cutscenes, 18 original story pieces of music, English and German.

**Lightsabers and combat**

- Ignition, glow, hum, trails and sparks; 14 hilts, 6 adjustable blade colors and 15 fighting styles.
- Guard, parry, dodges, spin attack and bolts turned back with the blade; Force push, grip, pull and jump.
- Only the swords you choose become lightsabers, and they turn back into exactly what they were.

**Starships, droids, blasters and more**

- X-Wing, TIE Fighter and TIE Interceptor to fly; Millennium Falcon and Star Destroyer to fly and walk through.
- 8 droid companions, 9 blasters that use arrows as ammunition.
- 11 film characters and 5 neutral outfits with original pixel skins, plus your own profiles.
- A space sky and 6 planet skies, 12 original pieces of music for the open world, voluntary duels and 11 advancements.

## Good to know

- Only Minecraft 26.2 with Fabric; no Forge, NeoForge or Quilt.
- Only Chapter 1 of the story exists so far. On a server each player plays their own copy of the story.
- Starships, droids and blasters are the newest parts and have not been balanced with many players yet.
- A server full of real players at once has not been tried yet.

All controls, commands and settings: [README](https://github.com/Phiro4241/SaberCraft#readme). Please report bugs with your Minecraft, Fabric Loader, Fabric API and SaberCraft versions and the log: [issues](https://github.com/Phiro4241/SaberCraft/issues).
