# SaberCraft 0.1.0

*Draft. Not published: no release, no tag.*

The first public version of SaberCraft, a Fabric mod for **Minecraft Java 26.2**: film-style lightsabers and a whole galaxy around them, with Chapter 1 of a story campaign.

SaberCraft is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Lucasfilm, Disney, Mojang or Microsoft. It contains no film music, no film sounds and no film artwork.

## Requirements

- Minecraft Java Edition **26.2**
- **Fabric Loader** 0.19.5 or newer
- **Fabric API** for 26.2 (built and tested with 0.161.0+26.2)

On a server, install SaberCraft and Fabric API on the server and on every client.

## What is in this version

**Story mode: Chapter 1, "Nightshade"**

- Six missions aboard the Star Destroyer *Tenebris*: the briefing aboard the Millennium Falcon, sneaking aboard, a disguise, the reactor, a discovery and a finale with an escape and a lightsaber duel against the *Shadowblade*.
- Stealth with detection stages, darkness, cover, crawl ways and silent takedowns; a stormtrooper disguise; dialogs with choices; two droid helpers; short cutscenes you can skip.
- The characters speak to you by your own Minecraft name.
- Checkpoints, three difficulties, 18 original story pieces of music, German and English texts.

**Sandbox**

- Lightsabers: ignition, glow, hum and swing sounds; 14 hilts, 6 blade colors, 15 fighting styles, guard, parry, dodges, spin attack, four Force abilities. Only the swords you choose become sabers.
- 11 film characters and 5 neutral outfits with original pixel skins, plus your own profiles.
- 9 blasters that use arrows as ammunition *(experimental)*.
- 8 droid companions *(experimental)*.
- X-Wing, TIE Fighter and TIE Interceptor to fly; Millennium Falcon and Star Destroyer to fly and walk through *(experimental)*.
- A space sky and 6 planet skies, 12 original pieces of music for the open world, voluntary duels and 11 advancements.

## Known limitations

- Only Minecraft 26.2 with Fabric. No Forge, NeoForge or Quilt.
- Only Chapter 1 of the story. On a server each player plays their own copy of the story (no co-op).
- Blasters, droids and starships are experimental: built and tested automatically, little played by hand, not balanced with real players.
- A server with several real players at once has not been tested yet.
- Shader packs: Complementary Reimagined keeps the planet skies pale; other packs are untested.
